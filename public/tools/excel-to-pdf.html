Excel to PDF online

Render XLS/XLSX worksheets into a paginated PDF. Choose a workbook and select Convert & download. Complex formulas and formatting may not be preserved exactly.

How to use Excel to PDF

  1. Select XLS/XLSX files from your device.
  2. Choose the options shown by the tool and preview or confirm the result.
  3. Download the finished file and check it before sharing or publishing.

What this tool is best for

Convert Excel spreadsheets to paginated PDF files in your browser. Supports common XLS and XLSX workbooks. IMAGE 24 is designed for common everyday files and browser-first processing. The application limit is 50 MB per selected file.

Important limitations

Browser conversions can behave differently for complex layouts, scanned documents, embedded fonts, protected files or image-heavy PDFs. This tool does not bypass passwords or security controls. Keep an original backup of important documents.
